# TOWER DEFENSE
 - Final name TBD -

### Abstract
This game is an asymmetrical cooperative tower defense board game with deck building mechanics and character leveling up. 

### Theme / Lore
Set in a world resembling Earth, animal heroes face waves of world-destroying enemies. To prevent them from ravaging the world, and capturing its protectors — the Wights —, heroes must unite and repell them. Adversaries come in various shapes: Oil companies digging and fragilizing the ecosystem, Agriculture companies cutting the trees and contaminating the soils, etc.

This game intends to be apolitical in the way the lore and the cards are written, even if the idea is to raise awareness.

### A combo-driven game
This tower defense heavily relies on combo mechanics to defeat waves of increasingly challenging foes : engine building ; multipliers and scaling ; and synergies inside the deck and between players.  

### Inspiration
- Board games: Aeon’s End, Imperium: Legends, Dead Cells, Hero Realms, Spirit Island (for hero meta), The Witcher (for discard mechanics and XP), Zombicide (for XP progression), Dune:Imperium
- Video games: Balatro, Black Jacket, Monster Train, Slay the Spire, Dead Cells 

### Gameplay
Players choose an animal hero with its own starting deck, favorite terrain(s), mechanics, etc. To face ever bigger enemies, they can buy cards from the market and unlock new cards – shared or player-specific – by completing contracts or kicking out elite mobs.

To discard is to sow, to play is to reap. To play cards will cost.

### Game modes
This game is intended to be first played in campaign mode, with a set of pre-written scenarios. Then, players can design their own.
