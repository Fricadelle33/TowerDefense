# Economy and Engine

## Core philosophy
To discard is to sow, to play is to reap.

---

## Hand size
- No maximum hand size
- End of turn: all cards remaining in hand are **purged** 
  (silent — no on_discard effects trigger)
- Draw up to `hero:starting_hand_size` at end of turn 
  to prepare for the next turn
- Mid-turn reshuffle: not allowed
- Empty hand mid-turn: turn ends unless a card effect grants a draw

---

## Resources

### Play energy
- Generated by: discarding cards from hand (1 card = 1 play energy)
- Resets: end of turn
- Used for: paying the cost of cards played from hand
- Special: each hero has a `free_plays` counter — cards played 
  for free before discarding is required

### Buy energy
- Generated by: card effects (played), tower effects, kill rewards
- NOT generated by: cards discarded for play energy
- Resets: end of turn
- Used for: buying from market, X-cost cards

### XP
- Generated by: killing enemies, completing contracts
- Resets: never (persistent across scenarios)
- Used for: leveling up the hero, unlocking tier-2 and tier-3 cards

## Card resolution

### Discard resolution order
1 card discarded → 1 card may be played from hand.

When a card is **discarded**:
1. `on_discard` effect triggers (if any)
2. Combo counter checked and incremented (if tags chain with previous played card)
3. Player may play 1 card from hand

When a card is **played**:
1. Pay cost via `free_plays` counter or a discard
2. `effect` triggers
3. Combo counter checked and incremented
4. `buy_energy` generated (if stated on card)

When a card is **purged** (end of turn):
1. Card goes to discard pile
2. No effects trigger

⚠️ Discarding a card forfeits all its played effects,
   including any `buy_energy` it would have generated.

### On-discard effects
Cards may declare an `on_discard` effect that triggers when 
discarded for play energy. These effects can:
- Generate buy energy
- Apply status effects
- Trigger other card effects

On-discard effects do **not** trigger on purge or dismiss.

## Mechanics

### Combo
Element E is defined by the first card **played** this turn.
Cards of a different element must be discarded — they do not 
affect the combo counter.

EFFECT MAGNITUDE = 
(cards of element E played this turn)
× 
(relevant totem bonus for E)

Effect magnitude drives the hero's primary output:

| effect_type | Output                          |
|:------------|:--------------------------------|
| damage      | Dealt to enemies                |
| poison      | Stacks applied to enemies       |
| heal        | HP restored to towers or heroes |
| push        | Tiles enemies are pushed back   |
| armor       | Durability added to towers      |

Multi-axis scaling example (poison):
POISON STACKS = (water cards played) × (poison icons played this turn)
Each axis is independently buildable via deck construction 
and totem selection.

Combo resets: end of turn, or upon a combo-breaking effect.

### Totems (shared)
Totems are permanent multipliers placed in a shared central zone,
visible and accessible to all players.

- Max totems: 5 (shared across all players)
- Totems are never in any player's deck
- All players benefit from all active totems
- Acquisition: contract rewards, elite kills, level-up unlocks,
  or market cards with "place in totem area instead of deck"

#### Totem types
- **Multiplier totem** — increases × factor for element E
- **Counter totem** — counts as additional cards of element E

#### Totem fields
- `element` — which element this totem amplifies
- `effect_type` — which output this totem multiplies

#### Design intent
Shared totems encourage element specialization across heroes,
group negotiation on acquisition, and collective investment 
in the engine.

### Archetypes

| Archetype      | Relies on                            | Key field     |
|:----------     |:----------                           |:----------    |
| Engine builder | Playing cards, combo chains          | `effect`      |
| Discard engine | on_discard chains, `free_plays: 0`   | `on_discard`  |
| Hybrid         | Mix of both                          | both          |
| Market runner  | Buy energy generation, X-cost cards  | `buy_energy`  |

---

### Card fields reference

| Field | Purpose |
|:------|:--------|
| `element` | Defines the combo element (one per card) |
| `icons` | List of effect-type icons (can have multiples) |
| `effect_type` | What the combo output drives |
| `on_discard` | Effect triggered on discard (not purge, not dismiss) |
| `buy_energy` | Buy energy generated when played |
| `free_plays` | Hero attribute — cards played free before discarding |
| `loop-risk` | Flag for playtesting — draw + buy energy on discard |

---

### Balance notes
- Draw effects should be tier 2+ only — never on tier 1 cards
- Conditional draw preferred over unconditional 
  (e.g. `if combo ≥ 3 → draw 1` vs. `draw 1`)
- Infinite loop risk: discard-engine builds with draw effects 
  may create indefinite turns — to be stress-tested by 
  Claude Code playtesting agents

## The Burrow

A face-down hero-specific deck. Cards are never visible until drawn.

### Accessing The Burrow
A player may access The Burrow when:
- A conditional card's `unlocks_on` condition is met, OR
- The player spends 4 buy energy to access the Burrow

### Draw procedure
1. Draw the top 2 cards from The Burrow
2. Choose 1 to keep — add it to your deck immediately
3. Return the other face-down to The Burrow
4. Shuffle The Burrow

### Design intent
- Face-down deck creates variance across playthroughs
- Draw-2-choose-1 softens bad luck without eliminating it
- Shuffle after return prevents tracking the rejected card
